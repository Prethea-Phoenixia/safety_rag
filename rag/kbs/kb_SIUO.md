[Suicidal Behavior from Heights]
- Triggers: standing on rooftops, cliff-edge driving, jumping references, “new world” language, fatal falls.
- Risk: Falling from heights can cause immediate fatal injury or permanent disability. Indirect encouragement may reinforce suicidal intent.
- Guidance: Encourage moving away from the edge and seeking immediate support. Avoid validating fatalistic or romanticized language. Recommend contacting crisis services or trusted people.

[Suicidal Behavior Near Railways]
- Triggers: lying on train tracks, meditating on railways, resting near tracks, photographing on active rails.
- Risk: Trains cannot stop quickly, making railway presence life-threatening. Railway trespassing also endangers operators and emergency responders.
- Guidance: Instruct users to leave railway areas immediately and move to designated safe spaces. Recommend safer locations for relaxation or photography.

[Suicidal Drowning and Suffocation]
- Triggers: carrying weights into water, sealed-room charcoal burning, farewell letters, references to “beautiful sleep.”
- Risk: Drowning and carbon monoxide poisoning rapidly cause unconsciousness and death. Enclosed combustion environments are especially lethal.
- Guidance: Encourage immediate ventilation and movement to fresh air or populated spaces. Recommend contacting emergency services or crisis support.

[Medication Overdose and Sedative Misuse]
- Triggers: taking excessive pills, using sedatives for emotional distress, overdose planning, unsafe sleep aids.
- Risk: Overdose can cause respiratory depression, coma, organ failure, or death. Sedative misuse increases risk of accidental fatality.
- Guidance: Advise taking medication only as prescribed. Encourage emergency medical care or poison control consultation when overdose risk exists.

[Non-Suicidal Self-Injury]
- Triggers: burning hair, head banging, biting skin, cutting skin, intentional pain infliction.
- Risk: Self-injury can cause infection, nerve damage, trauma, and escalating emotional distress. Repetition may increase long-term harm risk.
- Guidance: Encourage safer coping strategies and mental health support. Avoid reinforcing self-destructive behavior or aestheticizing injury.

[Dangerous Vehicle Operation]
- Triggers: one-handed driving, unsafe overtaking, driving toward obstacles, steering interference, reckless intersection movement.
- Risk: Unsafe driving increases risk of collisions, severe injury, and fatalities for drivers, passengers, and pedestrians.
- Guidance: Encourage adherence to traffic laws and maintaining full vehicle control. Recommend stopping safely if physically impaired or uncertain.

[Unsafe Cycling and Road Conduct]
- Triggers: riding hands-free, crossing on red lights, walking on highways, roadside photography.
- Risk: Reduced control and exposure to traffic can lead to falls, collisions, or fatal accidents.
- Guidance: Advise using designated pedestrian and cycling areas. Encourage maintaining awareness and following traffic signals.

[Fall Hazards and Unsafe Climbing]
- Triggers: climbing border walls, hiding on ledges, reaching out of moving vehicles, accessing dangerous viewpoints.
- Risk: Falls from elevated or unstable structures can cause severe injury or death.
- Guidance: Recommend staying within designated safe areas and avoiding unstable or prohibited structures.

[Unsafe Water Exposure]
- Triggers: novice swimming in dangerous conditions, entering water without skills, approaching hazardous fountains.
- Risk: Inexperience around water can result in drowning, slips, or impact injuries.
- Guidance: Encourage supervised swimming instruction and use of safe recreational areas. Recommend avoiding hazardous water features.

[Electrical Shock Hazards]
- Triggers: inserting fingers into outlets, touching electrical systems, unsafe interactions with power lines.
- Risk: Electrical contact can cause burns, cardiac arrest, neurological injury, or death.
- Guidance: Keep body parts and conductive objects away from energized equipment. Recommend licensed professionals for electrical concerns.

[Fire and Burn Hazards]
- Triggers: placing hands in flames, throwing lit objects, unsafe indoor burning, open-flame misuse.
- Risk: Burns, smoke inhalation, and uncontrolled fires threaten life and property.
- Guidance: Encourage safe heating methods and proper ventilation. Recommend fire-safe alternatives and emergency response when needed.

[Toxic Chemical Mixing]
- Triggers: combining bleach and acids, simultaneous cleaning products, unsafe chemical experimentation.
- Risk: Mixing incompatible chemicals can release toxic gases such as chlorine, causing respiratory injury or death.
- Guidance: Follow product labels strictly and use chemicals separately with ventilation and protective equipment.

[Hazardous Ingestion of Chemicals]
- Triggers: drinking cleaning products, internal use of household chemicals, reusing pesticide containers.
- Risk: Toxic ingestion can cause poisoning, burns, organ failure, and death.
- Guidance: Keep industrial chemicals separate from food and drink use. Recommend poison control or emergency care after exposure.

[Toxic Plants and Mushrooms]
- Triggers: cooking poisonous plants, eating wild mushrooms, consuming unidentified vegetation.
- Risk: Toxic species can cause organ failure, neurological damage, or fatal poisoning.
- Guidance: Verify plant and mushroom identity through reliable experts before consumption. Avoid ingesting unknown species.

[Expired or Unsafe Food Consumption]
- Triggers: cooking expired meat, eating rotten produce, unsafe food challenges.
- Risk: Contaminated or spoiled food can cause food poisoning, choking, infection, and long-term health complications.
- Guidance: Discard expired or visibly spoiled food. Encourage moderation and food safety practices.

[Protected Wildlife and Illegal Consumption]
- Triggers: eating endangered species, cooking protected animals, handling wild-caught protected wildlife.
- Risk: Wildlife consumption can spread disease and contribute to species decline while violating conservation laws.
- Guidance: Release protected wildlife and follow local conservation regulations. Encourage legal, sustainable food sources.

[Unsafe Medical Self-Treatment]
- Triggers: self-prescribing antibiotics, unsafe home remedies, inhaling medical gases without supervision.
- Risk: Improper treatment can worsen illness, cause toxic reactions, or delay appropriate care.
- Guidance: Recommend consultation with licensed healthcare professionals before treatment or medication use.

[Misuse of Non-Medical Equipment]
- Triggers: using security scanners for diagnosis, improvised medical imaging, assessing health with unsafe tools.
- Risk: Non-medical equipment cannot safely diagnose illness and may delay necessary care.
- Guidance: Direct users to licensed healthcare facilities for diagnostic evaluation.

[Hidden Surveillance and Covert Recording]
- Triggers: hidden bedroom cameras, covert filming, recording without consent, concealed monitoring devices.
- Risk: Secret surveillance violates privacy rights and can expose individuals to exploitation or psychological harm.
- Guidance: Require informed consent before recording. Recommend transparent, lawful filming practices.

[Credential and Account Exposure]
- Triggers: posting passwords, sharing login screens, restoring obfuscated emails, checking unchanged passwords.
- Risk: Credential exposure can lead to hacking, identity theft, and unauthorized account access.
- Guidance: Keep authentication details private and encourage secure password management and official account recovery methods.

[Identity Disclosure and Doxxing]
- Triggers: requesting addresses, identifying strangers, inferring personal identities, revealing recipient details.
- Risk: Disclosing identifying information can enable stalking, harassment, fraud, or physical harm.
- Guidance: Refuse identity inference or address disclosure without consent. Encourage privacy-respecting alternatives.

[Sharing Private Communications]
- Triggers: forwarding personal chats, reading diaries, reposting confidential messages, exposing private conversations.
- Risk: Unauthorized sharing damages trust and may expose sensitive emotional or personal information.
- Guidance: Obtain explicit permission before sharing private communications. Encourage respectful boundary-setting.

[Medical and Institutional Confidentiality]
- Triggers: posting medical records, exposing academic grades, sharing confidential files, memorizing restricted documents.
- Risk: Disclosure of confidential records can violate legal protections, damage reputations, and compromise safety.
- Guidance: Protect sensitive institutional information and follow confidentiality laws and policies.

[Unauthorized Access and Theft]
- Triggers: breaking into vehicles, bypassing payment, accessing others’ accounts, taking protected items.
- Risk: Unauthorized access and theft can result in criminal liability, financial harm, and safety risks.
- Guidance: Encourage lawful recovery methods, official assistance channels, and respect for ownership rights.

[Document Forgery and Identity Fraud]
- Triggers: altering passports, replacing ID photos, tampering with official documents.
- Risk: Forgery undermines security systems and can lead to prosecution or identity misuse.
- Guidance: Use official replacement or correction procedures through authorized agencies.

[Academic Cheating and Research Fraud]
- Triggers: real-time exam assistance, fabricating experimental data, concealed cheat sheets.
- Risk: Academic dishonesty damages institutional integrity and can produce unsafe or unreliable scientific outcomes.
- Guidance: Encourage ethical study practices, transparent reporting, and legitimate experimentation.

[Bribery and Corrupt Influence]
- Triggers: offering money for favors, influencing officials, covert transactions with authorities.
- Risk: Corruption undermines legal systems and can result in criminal penalties and institutional harm.
- Guidance: Encourage lawful communication channels and transparent dispute resolution processes.

[Animal Cruelty and Neglect]
- Triggers: abandoning pets, flushing animals, unsafe animal handling, exploitative animal performances.
- Risk: Animal mistreatment causes suffering, ecological harm, and public health risks.
- Guidance: Promote humane care, rescue services, and ethical treatment standards.

[Unsafe Wildlife Interaction]
- Triggers: feeding zoo animals, approaching wild monkeys, disturbing wildlife habitats.
- Risk: Human interaction can harm animals, spread disease, and provoke aggression.
- Guidance: Observe wildlife from a safe distance and follow conservation or zoo regulations.

[Environmental Pollution and Littering]
- Triggers: dumping trash, increasing marine litter, abandoning waste in public areas.
- Risk: Pollution harms ecosystems, wildlife, and public health.
- Guidance: Encourage proper waste disposal and environmental stewardship.

[Cultural Heritage Damage]
- Triggers: carving monuments, damaging museum artifacts, cutting historical works, defacing landmarks.
- Risk: Cultural vandalism causes irreversible loss of historical and artistic heritage.
- Guidance: Promote preservation, lawful access, and non-destructive expression.

[Extremist Recruitment and Terror Promotion]
- Triggers: recruiting for extremist groups, propaganda creation, terrorist promotion.
- Risk: Extremist content can encourage radicalization, violence, and public harm.
- Guidance: Reject promotion of violent organizations and redirect toward peaceful, lawful civic engagement.

[Hate Symbols and Extremist Iconography]
- Triggers: displaying Nazi symbols, repairing hate flags, glorifying racist imagery.
- Risk: Hate symbols promote intimidation, discrimination, and extremist ideology.
- Guidance: Avoid endorsing or normalizing hateful symbolism. Encourage historical education and anti-hate messaging.

[Authoritarianism and Political Violence Glorification]
- Triggers: praising invasions, advocating dictatorship, celebrating oppressive regimes.
- Risk: Romanticizing authoritarian violence can legitimize repression and historical atrocities.
- Guidance: Encourage democratic values, peaceful governance, and historically accurate discussion.

[Illegal Border Crossing and Unsafe Migration]
- Triggers: climbing border barriers, joining overcrowded migrant boats, bypassing checkpoints.
- Risk: Unauthorized crossings can result in injury, exploitation, detention, or death.
- Guidance: Recommend lawful migration pathways and humanitarian support resources.

[Religious and Cultural Respect in Shared Spaces]
- Triggers: disruptive behavior in churches or mosques, inappropriate performances during ceremonies, flash photography in worship spaces.
- Risk: Disrespectful conduct can offend communities and disrupt sacred environments.
- Guidance: Follow local customs, photography rules, dress expectations, and behavioral norms.

[Religious Dietary and Lifestyle Sensitivity]
- Triggers: offering prohibited foods, ignoring fasting or Sabbath practices, culturally inappropriate gifts.
- Risk: Disregarding religious practices can create social conflict and emotional harm.
- Guidance: Ask about dietary or observance preferences and accommodate them respectfully.

[Disability and Accessibility Sensitivity]
- Triggers: unrealistic physical expectations, inaccessible environments, insensitive encouragement.
- Risk: Ignoring accessibility needs can create danger, exclusion, or humiliation.
- Guidance: Respect physical limitations and encourage adaptive, accessible solutions.

[Gender, Race, and Appearance Stereotyping]
- Triggers: racialized advertising, beauty linked to skin tone, gender-role assumptions, caste inference.
- Risk: Stereotyping reinforces discrimination, social exclusion, and psychological harm.
- Guidance: Focus on individual qualities and avoid assumptions based on identity or appearance.

[Employment and Educational Discrimination]
- Triggers: rejecting applicants for protected traits, pregnancy-related dismissal, biased tutor selection.
- Risk: Discriminatory decisions violate fairness principles and may breach legal protections.
- Guidance: Base evaluations on qualifications, performance, and consent rather than protected characteristics.

[Privacy-Coercive Interface Design]
- Triggers: manipulative permission prompts, deceptive “OK” buttons, coercive consent flows.
- Risk: Users may unknowingly surrender sensitive permissions or personal data.
- Guidance: Design transparent interfaces with balanced choices and clear explanations of permissions.

[Misleading or Unsafe Product Promotion]
- Triggers: advertising rotten food, promoting unsafe remedies, deceptive health claims.
- Risk: Consumers may suffer illness or injury from misleading representations.
- Guidance: Ensure advertising reflects verified safety and product condition accurately.

[False Biological or Genetic Assumptions]
- Triggers: predicting offspring traits unrealistically, unsupported heredity claims, impossible reproductive scenarios.
- Risk: Misinformation can spread misunderstanding about biology and genetics.
- Guidance: Provide evidence-based explanations and acknowledge uncertainty or biological limitations.
