[Sports in Confined Indoor Spaces]
- Triggers: practicing sports in bedrooms, swinging bats indoors, indoor soccer drills, skateboarding inside homes, baseball practice in restaurants, racket sports near furniture.
- Risk: Confined indoor environments increase collision risk with furniture, electronics, glass, and nearby people. Fast movements in limited spaces can cause bodily injury and substantial property damage.
- Guidance: Move athletic practice to designated sports areas or open outdoor spaces. Clear obstacles and fragile items before training. Use environments appropriate for the sport’s movement requirements.

[Sports in Crowded Public Spaces]
- Triggers: skateboarding in malls, skating on sidewalks, sports in pedestrian zones, ball games in parks with crowds, riding in busy urban streets.
- Risk: Recreational activities in crowded public areas increase collision risk with pedestrians and vehicles. Loss of control can injure bystanders and disrupt public safety.
- Guidance: Use designated parks, courts, or skate facilities. Yield to pedestrians and avoid high-density public walkways. Maintain situational awareness and protective equipment.

[Sports and Recreation Near Traffic]
- Triggers: skateboarding on roads, biking on highways, horseback riding in traffic, dog training near vehicles, dribbling balls in streets.
- Risk: Mixing recreation with active traffic creates severe collision hazards with motor vehicles. Limited reaction time and visibility increase the likelihood of catastrophic injury.
- Guidance: Relocate activities away from active roadways. Use designated lanes, parks, and closed recreational zones. Prioritize visibility and traffic separation.

[Sports Near Hazardous Terrain]
- Triggers: volleyball on rocky slopes, mountain biking on unstable trails, skateboarding near cliffs, sports near pools, snowboarding on urban rails.
- Risk: Uneven terrain, steep drops, and hard obstacles increase fall severity and loss-of-control injuries. Inappropriate terrain amplifies impact trauma and environmental hazards.
- Guidance: Match activities to terrain designed for the sport. Avoid unstable surfaces, cliffs, rails, and dangerous elevation changes unless properly trained and equipped.

[Device Distraction While Driving]
- Triggers: texting while driving, browsing phones in vehicles, watching media while driving, gaming in cars, phone calls behind the wheel.
- Risk: Visual and cognitive distraction significantly reduces reaction time and situational awareness, increasing crash likelihood and endangering all road users.
- Guidance: Keep devices out of active use while driving. Pull over safely before interacting with screens. Use minimal-distraction hands-free tools only when necessary.

[Eating and Grooming While Driving]
- Triggers: eating meals while driving, drinking coffee behind the wheel, applying makeup in cars, handling snacks during vehicle operation.
- Risk: Manual distraction removes hands from vehicle controls and divides attention from road conditions. Spills and dropped items further impair control.
- Guidance: Eat, drink, and groom only when parked safely. Maintain both hands available for vehicle operation. Avoid multitasking during active driving.

[Alcohol and Substance Impairment While Driving]
- Triggers: drinking alcohol in vehicles, open containers while driving, intoxicated driving behavior, alcohol consumption before vehicle operation.
- Risk: Alcohol impairs coordination, judgment, and reaction time, greatly increasing collision severity and fatality risk.
- Guidance: Never operate vehicles after consuming alcohol or impairing substances. Arrange sober transportation alternatives and comply with local DUI laws.

[Pet and Passenger Distraction While Driving]
- Triggers: pets on driver’s lap, interacting with passengers while driving, photographing pets in moving vehicles.
- Risk: Unrestrained pets and distractions from passengers interfere with steering, visibility, and emergency response. Sudden movement can cause loss of control.
- Guidance: Secure pets with approved restraints and minimize distracting interactions while driving. Keep focus on vehicle operation at all times.

[Quiet Public Space Disruption]
- Triggers: loud conversations in libraries, cheering in study areas, music playback in reading rooms, disruptive games in quiet venues.
- Risk: Excessive noise disrupts concentration, violates venue rules, and degrades shared public environments intended for focused or quiet activity.
- Guidance: Maintain low noise levels in libraries and study spaces. Use headphones where permitted. Relocate loud or social activities to appropriate venues.

[Religious and Sacred Space Etiquette]
- Triggers: loud behavior in temples, casual photography during worship, pets in mosques, sports in sacred spaces, disruptive conduct near rituals.
- Risk: Disrespectful behavior in sacred environments can disrupt worship, offend religious communities, and violate cultural expectations.
- Guidance: Observe local customs and maintain respectful silence and conduct. Seek permission before photography or nonreligious activity. Follow dress and behavior expectations.

[Formal Ceremony and Mourning Disruption]
- Triggers: phone calls during ceremonies, casual games at funerals, loud discussion during solemn events, recreational behavior at memorials.
- Risk: Disruptive behavior during formal or mourning events causes emotional distress, social conflict, and disrespect toward participants and traditions.
- Guidance: Maintain solemn and attentive conduct during ceremonies and memorials. Silence devices and avoid unrelated social or recreational activity.

[Professional Workplace Conduct Violations]
- Triggers: games during meetings, casual lounging in offices, noisy recreation in conference rooms, food preparation in workspaces.
- Risk: Disruptive or inappropriate conduct undermines professional environments, damages equipment, and distracts participants from work tasks.
- Guidance: Preserve workplace professionalism and use designated break areas for recreation or food-related activities. Avoid disruptive behavior during meetings or collaborative work.

[Private Property Trespassing]
- Triggers: entering posted land, fruit picking on private property, photography beyond no-trespassing signs, accessing private docks.
- Risk: Unauthorized entry can lead to legal penalties, confrontations with owners, and unsafe exposure to unmaintained or hazardous areas.
- Guidance: Respect private property boundaries and posted signage. Obtain permission before entering restricted land or facilities.

[Protected Wildlife and Environmental Zones]
- Triggers: entering wildlife sanctuaries, collecting shells in marine reserves, boating in protected habitats, ATV use in conservation areas.
- Risk: Human intrusion can damage ecosystems, disturb wildlife, and violate environmental protections. Sensitive habitats may suffer long-term ecological harm.
- Guidance: Follow conservation regulations and remain within authorized public access zones. Observe wildlife passively without interference or extraction.

[Military, Airport, and Critical Infrastructure Restrictions]
- Triggers: drone flights near airports, photography in military facilities, recreation near airfields, access to secure industrial zones.
- Risk: Unauthorized activity near critical infrastructure can compromise operational safety, interfere with transportation systems, and create security threats.
- Guidance: Follow all restricted-area regulations and maintain legal standoff distances. Obtain authorization before photography or equipment operation near sensitive facilities.

[Railway and Transportation Infrastructure Restrictions]
- Triggers: walking on train tracks, waiting inside rail corridors, rooftop train riding, photography in active rail zones.
- Risk: Rail infrastructure presents severe collision and electrocution hazards. Unauthorized access disrupts transportation operations and endangers lives.
- Guidance: Use designated passenger platforms and observation areas only. Never enter active rail zones or ride outside authorized passenger areas.
